About

I am Mustafa Rehayee, a native speaker of Dari/Persian with a TOEFL iBT score of 101. I translate and interpret between Dari/Persian and English.

My work centers on clear, natural communication. I help English-speaking visitors talk with local communities, understand local information, and make sense of cultural context, so that meaning is not lost between languages.

Services

  • Script translation

    Video scripts, dialogue and subtitles, translated to read and sound natural.

  • Word and phrase translation

    Accurate wording for short texts, expressions and terms.

  • Document translation

    Written documents translated with care for meaning and tone.

  • English ↔ Dari/Persian translation

    Both directions, with attention to how each language is really used.

  • Proofreading and editing

    Review of existing Dari/Persian or English text for clarity and correctness.

  • Consecutive interpretation

    Spoken interpretation for English-speaking visitors and local communities in Afghanistan.
